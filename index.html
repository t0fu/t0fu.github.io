🫘 Tofu in Various Forms

A celebration of the world's most versatile bean curd.

🪨

Extra-Firm Tofu

Dense and sturdy — perfect for grilling, pan-frying, or cubing into stir-fries.

🍦

Silken Tofu

Smooth and creamy — ideal for soups, smoothies, desserts, and sauces.

🫙

Soft Tofu

Delicate and pillowy — wonderful in sundubu-jjigae (Korean soft tofu stew).

🧈

Firm Tofu

Versatile and holds its shape well — great baked, scrambled, or braised.

🫕

Braised Tofu

Simmered in savory sauces until it absorbs deep, rich flavors throughout.

🍢

Agedashi Tofu

Lightly battered and deep-fried, served in a delicate dashi broth.

🧀

Smoked Tofu

Infused with smoky depth — fantastic in salads, sandwiches, and tacos.

🍮

Tofu Pudding (Douhua)

Ultra-silky and served sweet with syrup, or savory with soy and ginger.

Made with ❤️ and a whole lot of bean curd.
